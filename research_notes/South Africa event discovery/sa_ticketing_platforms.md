# South African Event Ticketing / RSVP Platform Landscape (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-20. About 18 search/fetch calls. The IQ Magazine International Ticketing Report 2026 (SA chapter) returned HTTP 403 and could not be read. Many listicle and directory sources (Tracxn, ensun, aeroleads) are low quality, and they are flagged where used. The most current comparison found was published by TKKT, a competitor, so it is not neutral.

## Which ticketing/RSVP platforms are active in SA in 2025-2026, and what niches do they serve?

### Takeaway
SA ticketing has two layers.
- **Legacy retail-distribution incumbents:** Computicket (Shoprite/Checkers tills), Webtickets (Pick n Pay/Boxer) and Ticketpro (Blue Label; sport). They dominate stadium, sport, theatre and mass-market events where buyers pay cash in-store.
- **Self-service online platforms:** Quicket (now Ticketmaster-owned), Howler (festivals and dance, with cashless wristbands), plus many smaller low-fee entrants (uTickets, FestFlow, DigiTicket Africa, TicketWiz, EventSuite, Tixsa, TKKT launched 2026).

Global players also have a real foothold: Ticketmaster SA (since 2022) for arena tours, Fever (Candlelight concerts in Cape Town), and Resident Advisor (club and electronic nights in Johannesburg and Cape Town).

### Cited Findings

**Big three (context)**
- **Quicket.** Founded in 2011 in Cape Town. Self-service ticketing best known for general admission and festivals. Also operates in Nigeria, Uganda, Kenya, Zambia and Botswana — [Pollstar, 24 Jul 2024](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/07/24/ticketmaster-expands-business-in-africa-through-quicket-acquisition/)
- Quicket fees: 4.9% commission ex VAT plus R7.50 booking fee per ticket; free for free events. Described as best for workshops, community events and creators, with "strong organic discoverability" — [TKKT comparison, reviewed 30 Aug 2026 (competitor-authored)](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- **Howler.** Festivals, dance events and lifestyle brands. Known for cashless RFID wristband and on-site fintech tooling, and popular with younger audiences — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa). Recent Howler-ticketed events:
  - NYE Festival 2025 at Wild Waters, Boksburg — [Howler](https://www.howler.co.za/events/nye-festival-2025-f75e)
  - Ramfest x Halloween 2025, Pretoria — [Howler](https://www.howler.co.za/events/ramfest-x-halloween-2025-pretoria-b5b1)
  - GO! Festival in Johannesburg and Cape Town, Dec 2025, on a white-label subdomain — [gofestival.howler.co.za](https://gofestival.howler.co.za/events/go-festival-johannesburg-13-december-2025-89b1)
  - Our Origin Festival, Cradle of Humankind, 16–18 Oct 2026 — [Howler](https://www.howler.co.za/events/our-origin-festival-a837/tickets)
  - MTN Bushfire, Eswatini — [bush-fire.com](https://www.bush-fire.com/tickets/)
  - Co-ticketed fabric London's Jan 2026 shows in Johannesburg and Cape Town, together with Resident Advisor — [CEC Online](https://ceconline.co.za/fabric-london-returns-to-joburg-and-cape-town-in-january-2026/)
- Howler funding: Series B in 2020 from Techstars, Platform Investment Partners Growth and Platform45, to grow in Sub-Saharan Africa and expand to Europe and APAC — [BusinessTech](https://businesstech.co.za/news/?p=371840)
- **Webtickets.** Built for mass-market events that need retail ticket sales. Sells through the Pick n Pay and Boxer store network and reaches buyers who are not online. Its UX is more traditional — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- Webtickets in-store payment works at Pick n Pay, PnP Liquor, PnP Clothing, BP Express and Boxer. University of the Free State drama productions use it — [UFS](https://www.ufs.ac.za/humanities/departments-and-divisions/drama-and-theatre-arts-home/productions/how-to-book-for-productions)
- Webtickets also sells Table Mountain Cableway tickets via "Pay at Pick n Pay" (2019 post) — [Table Mountain](https://tablemountain.net/our-blog/askthecableway-how-do-i-collect-the-tickets-i-bought-online)
- Webtickets has a Namibian offshoot, "Webtickets Namibia" with WhatsOn Namibia — [The Namibian](https://namibian.com.na/?p=589554)

**Legacy incumbents beyond the big three**
- **Computicket.** Shoprite subsidiary, acquired in 2005 — [Shoprite 2005](https://www.shopriteholdings.co.za/articles/Newsroom/2005/shoprite-acquires-computicket.html). Sells in all Shoprite and Checkers stores, selected Usave and House & Home stores, and freestanding outlets — [Shoprite Holdings](https://www.shopriteholdings.co.za/group/brands/computicket.html)
- Computicket niches: major tours, sports and stadium events — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa). Examples: the Nedbank Cup final 2025 (whose sale crashed on 5 May 2025) — [Gauteng.net](https://www.gauteng.net/whats-on-g/nedbank-cup-final-tickets-heres-how-you-can-purchase-the-tickets/); the Soweto Derby (low-quality promo source) — [blog](https://sysmail2.onlinenic.com/blog/get-your-soweto-derby-2025-tickets-at-computicket-1764810971)
- **Computicket Box Office.** Launched in 2022 as a separate self-service site for small organisers (computicket-boxoffice.com). 4.5% commission, and free events list at no cost. Sold more than 130,000 tickets in its 2022 soft launch, mostly in stores — [Business Report, Jul 2022](https://businessreport.co.za/companies/2022-07-01-small-event-companies-can-now-sell-tickets-using-computicket-box-office); [Memeburn, Jun 2022](https://memeburn.com/2022/06/computicket-launches-box-office-self-service-platform/). Still in use in 2025 — [example listing](https://computicket-boxoffice.com/e/sa-awards-festival-2025-nENV8X)
- Theatre: Computicket is historically the main agent for Cape Town and Johannesburg theatre (Baxter, Artscape, Joburg Theatre) — [Lekkeslaap, undated](https://www.lekkeslaap.co.za/attractions/baxter-theatre); [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/africa-and-middle-east/south-africa/cape-town-and-peninsula/things-to-do/performing-arts). The Baxter also used Webtickets as of 2020 (self-refunds "on your Webtickets profile") — [UCT News, Mar 2020](https://news.uct.ac.za/article/-2020-03-17-baxter-theatre-centre-closed-until-end-april-2020). Current venue channels were NOT verified.
- **Ticketpro.** Part of Blue Label Telecoms since 2014. Official ticket agent for Cricket South Africa, the Lions, Blue Bulls and Sharks (rugby) — [LinkedIn](https://za.linkedin.com/company/ticketpro-za). Tracxn lists it as "deadpooled" — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/ticketpro/__y4yad8MDIWHS7j2ll7Esg_tydUzvcCJFmqut6Z8zGVs). That conflicts with its active social and LinkedIn presence and with TKKT (Aug 2026) listing it as operating in SA — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- Ticketpro has a Pick n Pay till integration: the till slip acts as the ticket, and buyers can reserve online and pay in-store within 48 h. It runs alongside Webtickets at PnP (article undated) — [ITNewsAfrica](https://www.itnewsafrica.com/industries/retail_news/retail-store-partners-with-electronic-ticketing-provider-to-offer-event-tickets-at-stores-nationwide/)
- **Plankton.** Official ticketing supplier for the Netball World Cup 2023 in Cape Town and criticised for ticketing problems. iTickets and TicketPro were among seven other bidders — [ABC News, Aug 2023](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-08-06/ticketing-nightmare-continues-at-netball-world-cup/102694390). Current status unknown.
- **iTickets.** Pretoria-based print-at-home ticketing for events and conferences. Its X account appears dormant — [X @iTicketsSA](https://x.com/iTicketsSA). Status uncertain.
- **Tixsa.** Centurion-based provider of online ticketing, box office, RFID scanning and event management — [tixsa.co.za](https://www.tixsa.co.za/services/online-ticketing). Listed as operating in SA in Aug 2026 — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)

**Global players**
- **Ticketmaster South Africa.** Launched on 27 Apr 2022 with a Cape Town HQ. First on-sales were Justin Bieber's Justice World Tour and Rugby World Cup Sevens 2022 — [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2022/04/27/ticketmaster-launches-in-south-africa/); [Ticketmaster Business](https://business.ticketmaster.com/press-release/ticketmaster-launches-in-south-africa). Serves "leading festivals, sports, music, theatre, and venue clients" — [Pollstar 2024](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/07/24/ticketmaster-expands-business-in-africa-through-quicket-acquisition/). Niche: international tours and arena shows — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- **Fever.** Active in Cape Town with Candlelight concerts at the Norval Foundation (Tokai) and Star Theatre. Prices run roughly ZAR 550–910. Themes include Vivaldi, Hans Zimmer, Queen vs ABBA, Adele, Coldplay and Brenda Fassie — [Fever](https://feverup.com/m/269468); [Fever](https://feverup.com/m/784747). Johannesburg presence was not confirmed in search results.
- **Resident Advisor (ra.co).** Has city guides with ticketed listings for Cape Town (ra.co/guide/za/capetown) and Johannesburg — [RA Cape Town guide](https://de.ra.co/guide/za/capetown). Used for club and electronic nights:
  - AND Club, Johannesburg: TOYTOY, In The Burg (1 Aug 2026)
  - fabric London JHB/CPT (Jan 2026), co-sold with Howler — [CEC Online](https://ceconline.co.za/fabric-london-returns-to-joburg-and-cape-town-in-january-2026/); [Texx and the City](https://texxandthecity.com/2026/01/fabric-london-unveils-killer-local-lineups-for-johannesburg-and-cape-town-shows/)
- **Eventbrite.** Listed as operating in SA — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa). Trip.com surfaces Eventbrite-sourced Tshwane events — [Trip.com](https://us.trip.com/events/5802770-tshwane-collection). No data found on SA scale.
- **Luma / Partiful.** Used for tech side-events (e.g. Africa Tech Festival, Cape Town, accepts "Luma, Partiful or Eventbrite" links for side events) — [altss](https://altss.com/conferences/africa-tech-festival). Tech meetups and conferences in Johannesburg and Cape Town are aggregated by dev.events — [dev.events JHB](https://dev.events/meetups/AF/ZA/Johannesburg/tech); [dev.events CPT](https://dev.events/meetups/AF/ZA/Cape_Town/tech)

**Smaller / newer local self-service platforms (all per [TKKT, Aug 2026](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa), a competitor-authored source)**
- **TKKT.** Launched 2026. R0 platform fee; payouts go straight to the organiser's own Yoco, PayFast or Paystack account. WhatsApp ticket delivery. Discovery marketplace is early-stage.
- **uTickets.** Flat 7% of cart (5% for trusted organisers, 4% for NPOs). Small and mid-size independents. Tracxn names Ticketmaster, Howler and Quicket as its competitors — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/utickets/__oZroIzVcvA14yzL6jEUzztNl3A8OtSZQhBHDblSyoKk)
- **FestFlow.** 3% (1.5% on premium option) plus about 2.9% via Paystack or Ozow. 48-hour batch payouts.
- **DigiTicket Africa.** 4.9–5.5% plus R5/R7.50 per ticket. Has its own discovery site; free and RSVP tickets carry no fees.
- **TicketWiz.** Conferences and delegate events: badges, attendance tracking. 4.9% plus R5.
- **EventSuite.** Festivals and large venues: accreditation, reserved seating, camping, parking. 5%.
- Tracxn also tracks **Dolazone** as an SA ticketing player — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/utickets/__oZroIzVcvA14yzL6jEUzztNl3A8OtSZQhBHDblSyoKk)

### Inferences
- Retail cash-at-till distribution is the SA-specific moat. Computicket (Shoprite/Checkers/Usave), Webtickets (PnP/Boxer) and Ticketpro (PnP tills) still own the mass-market, sport and township-reach segments. Online-only platforms own festivals, nightlife and community events.
- Nightlife and electronic events span Howler, RA and Quicket, often co-listed. Theatre is mainly Computicket and Webtickets. Sport is Computicket, Ticketpro and Ticketmaster. Tech meetups are Luma, Meetup and Eventbrite. Workshops and community events are Quicket. Classical and "experience" events are Fever.
- The long tail of low-fee entrants (TKKT, FestFlow, uTickets, DigiTicket) suggests continued fragmentation, which makes any single-source aggregator incomplete.

### Gaps
- **Unverified candidates:** I found no evidence in this session for Nutickets/Nutix, Kwanda, Ticketbox SA, Tickets.co.za, Evvnt, Showpass or DICE operating in SA. Treat them as unverified or absent and do not list them without confirmation.
- **Meetup and Partiful:** I found no SA-specific usage data.
- **Social and payment-link sales:** I found no sources on Yoco, SnapScan or PayFast payment-link ticket sales, or on Instagram/WhatsApp DM sales. They are widely assumed but not sourced here. TKKT's model of paying out to Yoco/PayFast/Paystack hints at this informal market.
- **Market shares and scale:** I found no reliable SA market-share numbers. A 2020 report calling Ticketpro "second-largest" relies on placeholders. The IQ 2026 ticketing report (SA chapter) was blocked (403).
- **Venue own-box-offices:** I could not verify current channels for Baxter, Artscape, Joburg Theatre or Ster-Kinekor (Ster-Kinekor sells its own cinema tickets, from general knowledge, unverified).
- **Running races and markets:** I did not research race-entry platforms (e.g. those used by parkrun-style or Comrades-type events) or market listing sites.

## Do they expose APIs, feeds, widgets, affiliate programmes, or browsable listings?

### Takeaway
None of the SA-native platforms publishes a clearly documented, public event-discovery API. Howler has a v3 consumer-portal API (cashless, top-up and cashout operations, plus webhooks), as indexed by third-party API catalogues. Quicket had an API used by a now-abandoned WordPress plugin. Ticketmaster (Discovery API) and Eventbrite have well-known public APIs. Most platforms do expose public, crawlable event pages; Howler, RA and Quicket have browsable upcoming-events listings.

### Cited Findings
- **Howler:** API at base URL `https://www.howler.co.za/api/v3/consumer_portal`, with cashless tag, top-up and cashout operations and webhooks for ticket and payment events. Auth via OAuth 2.0, JWT or API key. The source is a third-party catalogue, not Howler docs — [API Evangelist](https://providers.apievangelist.com/providers/howler/); [API Evangelist – cashout API](https://apis.apievangelist.com/store/howler-cashout-api/)
- Howler has a public "Upcoming events" listing page — [howler.co.za/upcoming](https://www.howler.co.za/upcoming) — and supports white-label organiser subdomains (e.g. gofestival.howler.co.za) — [Howler](https://gofestival.howler.co.za/events/go-festival-south-africa-registration-6483)
- **Quicket:** A "Quicket API Plugin" for WordPress "connects to the Quicket API to display a public feed of events". About 30 installs; last updated roughly 11 years ago and flagged "possibly abandoned" — [pluginsdb](https://pluginsdb.com/author/quijames). quicket.co.za/api returned 404 (checked 2026-10-20). No current official Quicket developer docs were found.
- Quicket (Aug 2026) pushes event distribution to Spotify, Google, Meta, Bandsintown and Apple Music, and is building WhatsApp ticketing and AI-powered search and purchase — [Live Nation news](https://news.livenationentertainment.com/news/ticketmaster-enters-east-africa-as-quicket-launches-in-uganda-and-kenya); [Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/ticketmaster-enters-east-africa-as-quicket-launches-in-kenya-and-uganda-n388749)
- **Ticketmaster:** Has a public developer portal (Discovery API) — [developer.ticketmaster.com](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/products-and-docs/apis/getting-started/). Whether SA inventory is exposed through it was not verified.
- **Eventbrite:** Has an API usable by third-party feed plugins with an Eventbrite API key — [WordPress plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/widget-for-eventbrite-api/)
- **RA:** Has public city guide pages for SA cities (Cape Town, Johannesburg) — [RA](https://de.ra.co/guide/za/capetown)
- **TKKT comparison:** The page contains no API information for any platform reviewed — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- **DigiTicket Africa:** Runs its own discovery site listing events — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)

### Inferences
- For an aggregator, the realistic sources are:
  - official APIs: Ticketmaster Discovery, Eventbrite, possibly Luma/Meetup
  - scraping or structured data (schema.org Event, likely present but unverified) on public listing pages: Howler /upcoming, Quicket, Computicket, Webtickets, RA guides, Fever
  - partnerships for the rest
- Quicket's distribution to Google and Meta implies structured event data exists, which may make it crawlable. This is inferred, not verified.

### Gaps
- Not verified for any platform: affiliate programmes, iCal/RSS feeds, embeddable widgets (Quicket and Howler likely have embeddable ticket widgets but this is unconfirmed), and robots/ToS restrictions on scraping.
- I did not check whether Computicket, Webtickets or Ticketpro listings are filterable by city or date via URL parameters.

## Market changes 2023-2026 (consolidations, entrants, failures)

### Takeaway
The defining consolidation is Ticketmaster (Live Nation) acquiring Quicket. The deal was announced on 24 Jul 2024; Quicket remains a standalone brand in Cape Town and was used as Ticketmaster's vehicle into Kenya and Uganda in Aug 2026. Other changes:
- low-fee self-service entrants keep arriving (TKKT in 2026, FestFlow, uTickets, DigiTicket)
- legacy incumbents had high-profile failures (the Plankton Netball World Cup 2023 problems; the Computicket Nedbank Cup 2025 crash)
- Computicket moved downmarket with its Box Office self-service product (2022)

### Cited Findings
- **Ticketmaster buys Quicket (announced 24 Jul 2024).** Undisclosed sum. Quicket keeps operating standalone in Cape Town under MD James Tagg. Seller Knife Capital had invested in 2017 via KNF Ventures — [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/07/24/ticketmaster-expands-business-in-africa-through-quicket-acquisition/); [News24](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/ticketmaster-the-worlds-largest-ticketing-group-buys-sas-quicket-20240724); [Africa PE News](https://www.africaprivateequitynews.com/p/ticketmaster-acquires-knife-capital)
- Both the Quicket and Ticketmaster brands still operate in SA as of Aug 2026 — [TKKT](https://tkkt.co.za/best-ticket-platforms-south-africa)
- **Quicket launches in Kenya and Uganda (Aug 2026).** Supports M-Pesa and Airtel Money. Early partners are the Beneath the Baobabs festival and EatOut. The launch is linked to AFCON 2027 venue upgrades — [AllAfrica, 26 Aug 2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608260457.html); [Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/ticketmaster-enters-east-africa-as-quicket-launches-in-kenya-and-uganda-n388749); [Music Week](https://www.musicweek.com/live/read/ticketmaster-s-quicket-expands-into-east-africa/094798)

### Inferences
- With Live Nation owning both Ticketmaster SA and Quicket, the market now has three corporate poles:
  - Live Nation (Ticketmaster + Quicket): arenas down to grassroots events
  - Shoprite (Computicket): retail and sport
  - independent Howler: festivals and cashless
- Webtickets, Ticketpro and a fragmented low-fee tail sit around them.

### Gaps

- Webtickets ownership was not confirmed.
- No news was found on Computicket changes after 2022, or on any other shutdowns. Plankton's and iTickets' current status is unknown.
